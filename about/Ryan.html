AboutMyBrother.txt

------------------------------------------------------------

If it wasn't already apparent to you, my brother is dead.

He died from a self inflicted wound on March 13th.
That's what detectives say.

Could have been a day sooner or later. They don't know.
We were gone to a concert. He didn't want to come.

I see why now...


My brother was a great guy - he really was.
He liked playing board games and going for walks and music.
He loved AJR, just like me.

His favorite color was blue and he'd always make a point to me that
aliens are real and that the assassination of JFK was an inside
job.

Don't ask me how those things are related. Everytime we talked about
our favorite colors he'd bring that up.

Odd, I know, but hey, he was full of suprises.


He was incredibly smart.
He got accepted to MIT with a full ride straight out of high school.

I wish I was like him in that way.

He never finished his degree.
Only got about a year into it before... well... before he died...


We never put up an obituary for him, or even had a funeral for that
matter.
My mom never thought it was necessary.

'We live in such a small town, hon. Everyone knew him here. Why waste
all that money setting one up?'

Yeah, sure thing.


I just think she coulden't handle the fact that her son was gone
forever - that she'd lost another child.

Either that, or she was afraid of Jason coming back.


Probably a bit of both.

Jason is our dad. After some rather unfortunate events, we no longer
see him. Probably for the better.


Yeah...


Anyways, that's a bit about my brother.
If you have any other questions about him, email me or something.

Thats all I've got.

- Evan
