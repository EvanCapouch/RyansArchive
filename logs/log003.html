------------------------------------------------------------

I keep telling myself there's got to be an explanation.

Minecraft is buggy. Mods break things. Saves get corrupted.
There has to be something I'm missing.

I've started writing everything down because I don't trust my memory anymore.
Not because I think I'm losing it... just because I keep second guessing what I actually saw.

The weird thing is that none of it is consistent.

Sometimes I load the world and nothing happens.
Sometimes I'll notice something that's just... wrong.

A torch missing.

A tree that I swear wasn't there.

Footsteps with no creatures around.

I don't know.

Maybe I'm paying too much attention.


I thought about recording some of it.

If I actually caught something on video I'd at least know I wasn't imagining things.

But every time something strange happens I just sit there trying to make sense of it instead of opening OBS.

By the time I think to record, it's already over.

So no videos yet.

Honestly... I'd rather figure this out myself before I start sounding crazy online.

For now I'll just keep saving screenshots and writing these logs.

If this turns out to be nothing, at least I'll know I wasn't completely making it up.
